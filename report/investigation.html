Investigation

When I was researching the Fantasy Premier League game, I came across accounts on social media recommending certain players in the game, and one who uses betting odds to calculate probabilities of teams winning or losing, or keeping clean sheets. I wanted to create a data-driven system to recommend certain players or teams for the game, by analysing their past performance. To start, I created a google form and circulated it among friends in my year, in order to gauge interest in my project plan.

Google form


Form results



This confirms that there is interest in my idea, and demand for such a thing. I looked up some of the websites, apps and social media accounts that my form users mentioned

Research from form

RobT FPL

He makes predictions about each week about goals scored, clean sheets etc, based on data from betting markets. This is different from what I want to do, but his data-driven approach is relevant.

FPL Harry

He posts videos explaining his team selections, and does appear to use some data when making predictions, but not systematically.

FPL Focal

His youtube channel is similar to fpl_harry, in that he posts weekly team recommendations and strategy advice. He does more extensive data analysis in his videos, some of which will be useful to my project.

FPL Updates&Tips

His posts are similar to RobT FPL, he makes predictions using betting market data. He also offers weekly team choice advice, and gives justifications based on data. He uses Fixture Difficulty Rating (FDR) data to advise users on their player choice. This is something that I want to implement in my recommendation.

Fantasy Football Pundit

This website includes information and tools for FPL users as well as information from betting markets, for the probabilities of events such as certain teams winning the league or certain players scoring in the next match. This is useful information, but is not what I'm aiming for. This website lacks the historical data analysis that I want to be the focus of my website.

FPL Data

This website includes a graph viewing facility, which I would like to incorporate into my website. It gives a good idea of what I want to achieve with my own website.


LiveFPL

This includes many options for analysis and planning. This website requires a user to input their FPL team ID, then gives advice and recommendations based on their current FPL team. This is an elegant solution and could be an option for future iterations of my project.


Premier fantasytools

This is a feature-packed website, containing various ways to analyse in-game teams, as well as giving predictions and recommendations. Many of these features are behind a paywall, so I don't know the functionality of them all.

FPL Statistics

This website provides a way to view predictions about goals and other stats in table or graph form. This is similar to what I'm aiming to achieve.

FPL Review

They offer paid and free options for team planning. After inputting my FPL ID, it gives me recommendations for what players to buy and sell over the next few weeks. This is useful, but not what I am seeking to do. While these are data-driven recommendations, it doesn't give the user any insight into why its making these recommendations. I want to provide data to the user in an understandable and legible format, so that they can make their own decisions based on the data.


Dataset

When searching for a suitable dataset, I came across several on kaggle and github, but they were all either out of date or in an unsuitable format.



I eventually found this github repository by vaastav, which contained up-to-date data in csv format. This is what I will be using for my project.
